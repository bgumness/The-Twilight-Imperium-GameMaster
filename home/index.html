A companion for Twilight Imperium · 4th Edition + Prophecy of Kings

Game­Master

One screen runs game night. The Ledger keeps the round, objectives and score. When the agenda phase arrives, the Council Chamber rises to run the vote. When fleets collide, the Battle Arbiter resolves the combat. Set up once and everything stays in step.

Saved in this browser on this device. No accounts, nothing sent anywhere.
How a game night flows

Three tools, one game

You mostly look at the Ledger. The other two appear when the game calls for them and step aside when they're done, carrying their results back to the score.

01 · Every round

The Ledger

The round console walks the strategy, action, status and agenda phases. Objectives, secrets and every other point land on one big score track, with turn clocks and a full game log.

Always on screen
02 · Agenda phase

The Council Chamber

Reveals each agenda with its art and a plain explanation, runs the vote with every player's abilities, and keeps the laws in force. Points it awards come back to the Ledger for you to confirm.

Rises when the agenda phase starts
03 · Tactical action

The Battle Arbiter

Resolves space and ground combat in place of dice, with every faction's techs, upgrades and abilities. A battle display can go on the TV. The result goes into the game log.

Rises from a combat step
Season

The Galactic record

Every finished game on this device: champions, who wins with what, and who never loses a battle.

Around the table

Screens and settings

Table screen

Open on a TV or second monitor. It shows the score track, round, speaker, objectives and laws, and updates live as the Ledger changes on this device.

Table options

Shared by all three tools. You can also change them during setup or from Game setup in the Ledger.

Saved games

Copy the whole game to move it to another device, or paste one in, including games from the old Claude version of the Ledger.
